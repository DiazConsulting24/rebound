Privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026. Applies to Rebound, operated by Nic Diaz (sole proprietor, Massachusetts, USA). Questions and requests: diazconsulting24@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your money information is used to run Rebound for you and the people in your household — nothing else.
  • We don't sell your information, share it for advertising, or show ads.
  • The AI assistant only sees your information after you agree, and never sees the records of a household member who hasn't agreed.
  • You can download everything and delete your account at any time.

What we collect

  • What you enter or import: accounts, balances, income, debts, bills, budgets, goals, credit report details and scores, letters and receipts you upload, tax items, mileage, and notes.
  • From banks you link (only if you link them): account names, balances and transactions, through Plaid or SimpleFIN Bridge. We never see or store your bank password.
  • Account details: your name, email address, sign-in passcode (stored only as a one-way hash), passkeys, and two-step sign-in settings.
  • Household details for the benefits finder (only if you fill them in): household size, state, income, and children's ages.
  • Technical records: a log of sign-ins and changes (date, device type, what changed) so your household can see who did what, and server logs kept for 30 days for security and troubleshooting.

Don't enter full account numbers, Social Security numbers or passwords. Rebound doesn't need them.

How we use it

  • To show your dashboard, plans, reminders and alerts, and to calculate payoff plans, budgets, credit and tax estimates.
  • To send the alerts you turn on (phone notifications, email).
  • To keep Rebound secure: detect sign-ins from new devices, limit repeated attempts, investigate problems.

The AI assistant

Rebound's AI features (questions, guided set-up, reading letters and receipts, drafting letters and call scripts, weekly moves) send the relevant parts of your information to the AI provider you or we connect — currently OpenRouter and the AI model provider it routes to. This happens only after you read and accept the AI notice. Each household member decides for themselves; records belonging to someone who hasn't agreed are left out. AI answers are educational, can be wrong, and aren't financial, legal or tax advice.

Who we share it with

  • People in your household you invite. Records you mark as private show only their balance to others.
  • Service providers who run parts of Rebound for us, under contracts limiting their use: hosting (Google Cloud), bank connections (Plaid, SimpleFIN Bridge), AI (OpenRouter and the model provider it routes to), email delivery, push notifications (Apple / Google / Mozilla push services, which see only an encrypted message).
  • When the law requires it, such as a valid court order. We'll tell you unless we're legally not allowed to.
  • If the business is sold, under the same promises in this policy.

We do not sell or "share" personal information as those words are defined in California law, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.

How we protect it

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) everywhere.
  • Sensitive settings (bank and AI connection keys) are encrypted with a key unique to each household. Backups are encrypted. Disks are encrypted at the hosting provider.
  • Passcodes are stored only as salted one-way hashes; passkeys and two-step codes are supported.
  • Limits on repeated attempts, automatic sign-out after inactivity, a written incident plan.
  • Weekly vulnerability scans, automatic security updates, and an independent security test before public launch.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

How long we keep it

While your account is open. When you delete your account, a household only you belong to is deleted right away, together with its encrypted backups. A shared household stays with its other members. Our service log of sign-ups, sign-ins and deletions is kept for one year; server logs for 30 days. Details are in our Data Retention and Disposal Policy, available on request.

Your choices and rights

  • Download all your data (Settings → Backups, or Account → Download my data).
  • Correct anything by editing it.
  • Delete your account and data (Account → Delete my account, or Settings → Erase all data).
  • Turn off the AI, bank links, emails and phone alerts at any time.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and other states), you may have more rights, such as knowing what we hold and appealing a decision. Contact diazconsulting24@gmail.com; we'll respond within the time the law sets.

Children

Rebound is for adults 18 and over. Household records can mention children (for example, for tax credits) but children don't get accounts.

Changes

We'll post changes here and tell you in the app before a significant change takes effect.
