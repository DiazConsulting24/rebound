Rebound

Get your money back on track, together.

Rebound helps households catching up on bills and debt see the whole picture: what's due, what's safe to spend, and the fastest way out of debt. It's built for couples and families who share the work.

Bills and reminders

Every bill on one calendar, reminders before they're due, and a warning when cash will run short.

Debt payoff plans

Snowball or avalanche plans with real interest rates, minimums and due dates, plus tools for collections.

Budgets that fit

See where the money goes, find subscriptions you forgot, and give every dollar a job.

Credit and help

Track credit scores, plan what moves them, and find tax credits and programs you may qualify for.

How Rebound treats your data

  • Bank connections are read-only. Rebound can never move money.
  • We never sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and each household has its own encryption key.
  • You can disconnect a bank, download your data or delete your account at any time.

Rebound is in private testing and opening to the public soon. Rebound gives general information to help you organize your finances; it is not financial, legal or tax advice.
